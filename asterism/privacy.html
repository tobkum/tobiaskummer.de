Privacy Policy of the game Asterism

Last updated: 12 October 2026

In short

Asterism collects no personal data. It goes online for one thing only: to buy or restore “the full sky” through Google Play.

In detail

No data collection. No personal data is collected, processed or shared with third parties. There is no account, no sign-in and no registration.

Internet access. The app holds the permissions android.permission.INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and Google Play’s BILLING, which Google Play Billing needs. They are used only to ask Google Play for the price of the full sky, to buy it and to check whether you own it. There is no server of mine: no backend, no accounts, no synchronisation. All puzzles ship inside the app, and the game plays offline.

No tracking, no ads, no analytics. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries are included.

Progress and settings. Solved puzzles, your ratings, the nights of "Tonight's sky" you solved (by date), a puzzle you left half-way, the last puzzle you opened, which rule cards you have already seen, how many times you have swiped a hex (until the swipe reminder retires), the Deep Sky skies you charted with your chosen law and depth and the order they are dealt in, how many hints you asked for on a puzzle in progress, whether you own the full sky, and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. If you have turned on your device's backup to your Google account, Android may include them in that backup, so they can be restored on a new phone or after reinstalling the app. This backup is made by Android and Google; I never receive it. You can turn it off in your phone's backup settings. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device.

Permissions. Apart from the three that Google Play Billing needs (above), the app requests no permissions: none for location, contacts, camera, microphone, storage, phone or vibration.

Google Play. The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data when you download or update it, under Google's privacy policy. Google Play may also show me aggregated, anonymous statistics about installs, crashes and app performance; these do not identify you.

Purchases. “The full sky” is a one-time purchase through Google Play. Google handles the payment under its own privacy policy; I never see your payment details. Google Play reports each order to me with the order information it gives developers (such as an order number, the product, the price, the date and the country). The app remembers on your device that you own the full sky, so it opens offline.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. The app stores no personal data with me; for a purchase, Google Play’s order information is all I have, and I can look up an order by its number if you write to me.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a version is released that changes anything described here.
