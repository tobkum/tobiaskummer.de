A logic puzzle for Android

A hex logic puzzle with no guessing.

Each number counts the stars touching its hex, as in Minesweeper, but every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. Place the last star and the stars you found join into a constellation.

Free to start. One purchase opens everything. No ads, no subscription.

Logic, not luck

Every puzzle goes through a solver that may only use what a player can deduce from the numbers. If it needs a single guess, it is not in the game.

Each move is checked as you make it: place every star by proof and the puzzle earns all three seals. Stuck? A hint rings the numbers that matter, then explains the step in plain words.

Seven worlds, seven rules

  • First Light: a number counts the stars touching its hex.
  • Horizon: a number at the edge counts the stars along its line.
  • Clusters: an arc shows whether a number’s stars sit together.
  • Bright Stars: a star can count the stars around it.
  • Still Water: the sky is mirrored, so every hex has a twin.
  • Binary Stars: stars come in touching pairs.
  • Nebulae: a glowing cloud counts the stars inside it.

Then the Zodiac: twelve signs that combine two rules each. Every world opens with a short card that teaches its rule.

Nightly puzzles and the deep sky

A new puzzle every night, gentle on Mondays and hardest on Sundays. Each one you solve lights a moon on the month’s calendar, gold for a flawless one.

Deep Sky holds over 4,000 more: pick a rule and a depth from shallow to deep. Sign in with Google Play Games for 35 achievements and a save that follows you to a new phone.

Free to start, one purchase for everything

  • Free: the first two worlds, tonight’s puzzle and the week before it, and 64 Deep Sky puzzles. Over 100 puzzles in all.
  • “The full sky”: one purchase, yours for good. Every world, every past night and all of Deep Sky, more than 4,500 puzzles.

Plays offline, without timers or lives. A Reduce motion setting calms every animation, and stars, dark sky and mistakes differ by shape as well as colour.
