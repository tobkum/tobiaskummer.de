A logic puzzle for Android

A hex logic puzzle with no guessing.

Each number counts the stars touching its hex, as in Minesweeper, but every puzzle can be solved by logic alone. Place the last star and the stars you found join into a constellation on an engraved sky atlas.

Free to start. One purchase opens everything. No ads, no subscription.

Logic, not luck

Every puzzle, hand-made or generated, goes through a solver that may only use deductions a player can make from the numbers on the board. A puzzle is only in the game if that solver finishes it without a single guess, so there is never a 50/50 at the end.

Every move is checked the moment you make it. A move the numbers already prove counts as proven; one made before it could be proven counts as lucky. Place every star by proof alone and the puzzle earns all three seals. Stuck? A hint first rings the numbers that matter, then marks the hex they decide, then explains the reasoning in plain words.

Seven worlds, seven ways to count

  • First Light: a number counts the stars touching its hex.
  • Horizon: a number at the edge counts every star along its line.
  • Clusters: an arc under a number shows whether its stars sit together or apart.
  • Bright Stars: a star can show how many stars touch it.
  • Still Water: the sky is mirrored or turned, so every hex has a twin.
  • Binary Stars: stars come in touching pairs, never alone, never three.
  • Nebulae: a glowing cloud counts the stars inside it.

Then the Zodiac: twelve signs that each combine two rules, three seasons and a grand finale. Each world begins with a short illustrated card that teaches its rule on a small board.

A puzzle every night, and the deep sky

There is a new puzzle every night, gentle on Mondays and hardest on Sundays. Each night you solve lights a moon on the month’s calendar, gold for a flawless one. No streaks, no reminders. Deep Sky holds over 4,000 more puzzles: pick a rule, a pair of rules or any rule, and a depth from shallow to deep.

Achievements and cloud saves

With Google Play Games, Asterism has 35 achievements, from your first star to two thousand Deep Sky skies, three of them hidden; what you solved before counts. Your progress is kept in your Google account, so another phone or tablet picks up where you left off, and progress from two devices is combined, never replaced. Signing in is optional: without it the game plays exactly as before.

Free to start, one purchase for everything

  • Free: the first two worlds (30 puzzles and 6 bonus puzzles), the first puzzle of each of the next five worlds, tonight’s puzzle and the six nights before it, and 64 Deep Sky puzzles. Over 100 puzzles in all.
  • “The full sky”, one purchase, yours for good: every world and the Zodiac (each opens as you reach it), every past night and all of Deep Sky. More than 4,500 puzzles, plus a new one every night. No subscription.
  • No ads, ever. No time limits, no lives. Plays offline; only the purchase goes through Google Play.

Made for a quiet moment

Swipe up on a hexagon for a star and down for dark sky, or tap and use the buttons. Notes let you try an idea before you commit to it. Numbers are set in a clear typeface with a plain, open zero; stars, dark sky and mistakes differ by shape as well as colour, and a Reduce motion setting calms every animation. Works on phones and tablets.
