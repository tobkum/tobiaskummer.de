Terms of the app Ribband

Last updated: 24 November 2026

In short

Ribband is free to download and to use. One optional purchase through Google Play, Pro, adds highlights and notes, saved searches, Listen and three more typefaces for good. There are no ads, no subscription and no account.

What is free

Without a purchase you can save links and articles from any app, read them offline, use the e-ink mode, keep order with Inbox, Archive, Shelves and tags, search the full text, import and export your library, and sync it through your own Google Drive. Ribband’s browser extension, which saves a page from your computer’s browser through that sync, is free as well. What is free now stays free.

Pro

What it adds. Pro is a one-time purchase. It adds highlights and notes (with export to Markdown or Obsidian), saved searches (searches kept under a name that show what matches now), Listen (articles read aloud by the speech voices on your device, with a queue; how they sound depends on the device) and three more reading typefaces: Source Serif, Merriweather and IBM Plex Sans. It is not a subscription and is never charged again.

Price and payment. The price is shown in Google Play before you buy. Payment, receipts and taxes are handled by Google Play under the Google Play Terms of Service.

Your device and Google account. The purchase belongs to the Google account you bought it with, and Pro needs a device with Google Play. On another device with Google Play and the same account, the app restores Pro by itself after it is installed, and “Restore purchase” in the app does it on request. If the Play Store app on a device uses another Google account, Pro is available there again once it uses the account Pro was bought with. The app remembers the purchase on your device, so Pro works offline as well.

Refunds and withdrawal. Refund requests go through Google Play under its refund policies. If Google Play refunds a purchase, Pro closes again in the app; your library is kept, and highlights and notes you made stay on your device. How the purchase works, your right of withdrawal and the model withdrawal form are on the page Purchase and refunds.

Your statutory rights as a consumer are not affected by anything on this page.

Using the app

You may use Ribband for your own, private use. The app, its texts and pictures are protected by copyright; please don’t copy or redistribute them.

A page you save, with the share menu or with the browser extension, is a copy for your own reading. Whether you may keep such a copy depends on the website and, for an article behind a sign-in, on your subscription and the website’s terms; that is your responsibility.

The app keeps your library on your device; what it stores, what it sends and why is described in the privacy policy.

Updates

Updates through Google Play fix problems and may add or change features. What Pro adds will not be taken away by an update.

Changes to these terms

If these terms change, the new version is published here with a new date before the update it belongs to.
