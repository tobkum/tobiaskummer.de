Ribband next to other read-later apps

Ribband is a read-later app for Android. It has no account of its own, the library is kept on the device, and Pro is one purchase. This page puts it next to the apps people most often compare it with, so you can see before installing whether it is the alternative you are looking for. Read on 25 November 2026 from each app’s own website and documentation. If something here is out of date, write to me and I correct it.

Matter runs on iPhone, iPad and the web and has no Android app. Readeck is open source and has its own Android app; it needs a Readeck server of your own, a hosted service is announced.

When another app fits better

  • You read on an iPhone, an iPad or in a browser tab. Ribband runs on Android only and has no web version. Instapaper, Readwise Reader and Matter have both.
  • You want your own server. wallabag, Karakeep and Readeck are built for that, and all three are open source.
  • You keep thousands of bookmarks and mostly want to organise them. Raindrop.io and Karakeep are bookmark managers first. Ribband is for reading what you keep.
  • You want feeds, newsletters or PDFs in the same app. Readwise Reader has them. Ribband does not.
  • You read on a Kindle. Instapaper sends digests to a Kindle with Premium, and Readwise Reader sends documents there. Ribband runs on Android e-readers, not on a Kindle.

When Ribband fits

  • You read on Android and do not want another account. Ribband asks for none, and nothing you save reaches me.
  • You do not want to pay every month. Saving, the reader, Inbox, Shelves, Archive, tags, full-text search, the e-ink mode, sync, import and export are free. Pro adds highlights and notes with export, saved searches, Listen and three more typefaces, for one purchase.
  • You read on an Android e-reader. The e-ink mode turns pages and uses the page keys.
  • You want the list to outlast the app. Export writes everything into one file, each article as a web page and as Markdown, and the format is documented.

Bringing your list along

Ribband imports the export files of Pocket, Instapaper and Omnivore, and browser bookmark files, which includes Raindrop.io’s HTML export. Links keep their titles, dates and tags. It does not import from Readwise Reader, wallabag or Matter yet.

Questions

Is there a read-later app for Android without a subscription?

Ribband is free to use, and Pro is one purchase. wallabag, Karakeep and Readeck cost nothing if you run them on your own server.

Which read-later apps work without an account?

Ribband has none of its own; only sync uses your Google account. The others on this page need an account, either with the service or on your own server.

Can I use Ribband together with one of them?

Yes. Nothing in Ribband depends on leaving another app, and its export can be read without it.
