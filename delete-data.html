Delete your data

How to delete the data the app Ribband has stored.

Last updated: 24 November 2026. This is a translation; the German version is the original.

Where the data is

Everything you save and do in Ribband is stored on your device. If you turn on sync, your library is also kept in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. None of it is sent to me. I hold no copy and so cannot delete anything for you: you delete all of it yourself, with the steps on this page.

Delete the copy in Google Drive

If you use sync on more than one device, first turn sync off on each of your other devices (in the app: Settings › Sync › Turn off or remove…). A device that still syncs copies its library into Google Drive again.

While sync is set up, in the app:

  • Open Settings › Sync.
  • Tap “Turn off or remove…”.
  • Choose “Delete synced data from Google Drive”. This deletes the copy and turns sync off.

Once sync is off, in Google Drive on the web:

  • Open Settings › Manage apps.
  • For Ribband choose Options › Delete hidden app data.

This deletes the whole copy: your links with their titles and article texts, tags and shelves, saved searches, highlights, notes, reading positions and the file with your device’s model name.

Remove Ribband’s access to your Google account

Turning sync off does not take back the access you gave Ribband to its hidden folder in your Google Drive. To remove it, open your Google Account (myaccount.google.com), go to Linked apps, choose Ribband and delete its connection.

The browser extension

If you use Ribband’s browser extension:

  • In its window, choose Sign out. This browser then no longer has access to your Google Drive.
  • Remove the extension from your browser. This deletes what it kept there: the account’s address, its access and the list of its own saves.

Pages it saved that no device has taken yet lie in the same hidden folder in your Google Drive and are deleted with the copy (see above). Removing Ribband’s access in your Google Account also ends the extension’s access.

Delete the data on the device

  • You delete single things in the app. They stay in “Recently deleted” for 30 days and can be deleted for good there at once.
  • You delete everything at once by uninstalling the app.

What is kept

The order information for a purchase of Pro that Google Play reports to me is kept as long as tax law requires. The purchase itself is held by Google in your Google account. A page report you sent me by e-mail is deleted within 12 months, or sooner if you ask me.
