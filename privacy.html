Privacy Policy of the app Masinop

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Sa madaling salita

Nasa phone mo lang ang listahan mo. Walang account, walang ads, at walang internet access ang app na ito. Wala kaming server na tumatanggap ng data mo.

In short

Masinop keeps your entries on your phone. It has no account, no ads and no permission to access the internet, and no server of mine receives your data.

In detail

Your money data. Masinop stores your entries, the people you add, their utang and payments, and your settings in the app's private storage on your phone. The database file is encrypted with a key kept in your phone's secure hardware. There is no account system, no sign-in and no registration.

No internet access. This version does not request the permission android.permission.INTERNET and therefore cannot connect to the internet. No analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries are included.

Google backup. If backup is turned on in your phone's settings and your phone has a screen lock, Android saves an end-to-end encrypted copy of the app's data, including the database key, in your own Google account, so that a new phone can get your list back. I cannot read this copy. If your phone has no screen lock, the app's data is not sent to Google backup.

Reminders and statements you share. Masinop never sends messages by itself. When you choose to share a reminder or a statement card, the app hands the text or picture to the app you pick (for example Messenger, Viber or SMS), and that app's own privacy policy applies. A saved statement picture goes to the place you choose on your phone.

Names of people. The names you type for people who owe you or whom you owe stay on your phone. The app does not read your contacts.

Permissions. The app requests no permission for location, contacts, camera, microphone, SMS, notifications, storage or phone.

Google Play. The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may process data when you download or update it, under Google's privacy policy. Google Play may also show me aggregated, anonymous statistics about installs, crashes and app performance; these do not identify you.

Purchases. This version contains no purchases. This policy will be updated before a version with purchases is released.

Record-keeping only. Masinop does not lend money, does not check credit and does not share anything with lenders.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Articles 15 to 21 GDPR) and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In the Philippines, the Data Privacy Act of 2012 gives you similar rights, and you can complain to the National Privacy Commission. As the app stores no personal data with me, there is usually nothing I could provide information about. To delete everything, uninstall the app; to remove the Google backup copy, manage backups in your Google account.

Changes

This policy will be updated before a version is released that changes anything described here.
